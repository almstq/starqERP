# starqERP identity research notes — 24 Aug 2026

Purpose: category-collision and implementation research, **not visual imitation**.

## Category audit
Reviewed public brand materials for Odoo, Xero, Sage, Zoho and QuickBooks/Intuit. Common category risks observed across ERP/accounting/SaaS identity work include strong single-colour wordmarks, badges/circles, generic finance/document icons, node networks and integration arrows. The starqERP mark was therefore developed without competitor geometry, logos, or tracing.

Chosen territory: **Confluence / Course** — three operational record lines converge into a single forward course. It maps directly to the product promise: separate workstreams become one operational record, then point the owner toward what comes next.

## Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA requires at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. Core starqERP text/background pairs were checked against those thresholds. The chart system uses separate light/dark variants and should also pair hue with markers or line styles for multi-series charts.

## Thaana
Noto Sans Thaana is available from Google Fonts/Noto, supports Thaana, has multiple weights, and is distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Use semantic `lang="dv" dir="rtl"`; do not mirror the product wordmark inside RTL interfaces.
