Wordmark · four treatments of one idea
The case shift is the whole mark
Lowercase starq is the house. Uppercase ERP is what this one does. Every treatment below must survive being set at 16 px and in one colour — that is the test, not how it looks at 60 px.
W1 Weight shift
starqERP
starqERP 16 px
Quietest. Reads as one word at every size and needs no colour to work. Weakest at a glance in a crowded list.
W2 Amber ERP
starqERP
starqERP 16 px
Most memorable. But amber is the primary-action colour in the product — using it here spends it twice, and the mark dies in one-colour print.
W3 House dominant
starqERP
starqERP 16 px
Built for a family. starqPay and the rest slot straight in. The product name recedes — right if starq is the brand you are building.
W4 System tag
starq ERP
starqERP 16 px
The mono ERP says "this is infrastructure". Most distinctive of the four. Risk: the two faces can read as two words at small sizes.
The test that decides it: squint at the 16 px row under each mark. That is the size it appears at in a browser tab, a sidebar and a favicon — which is where it will be seen ten thousand times more often than at 54 px.
