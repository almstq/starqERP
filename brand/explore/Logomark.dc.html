Logomark · four ideas, each proved small
A mark that survives 16 pixels and one colour
Each option is shown at 64 px, then at 32 and 16, then flat black on white — the state it will be in when a local printer puts it on a workshop sign or an invoice.
L1 The entry
Two ledger rules with one posted entry sitting on them. Literal about what the product is: everything gets written down. Holds at 16 px because it is two lines and a dot.
L2 The join
Two flows meeting and continuing as one — which is literally what the product does to customers, work, stock and money. Most conceptual. Thickens acceptably at 16 px.
L3 Fix
A sextant angle — taking a bearing to know where you are. Maldivian seafaring without drawing a boat or a star. Risk: at a glance it can read as a generic upward chart arrow.
L4 The tail
The q of starq, reduced to a bowl and a descender. The only option drawn from the name itself, so it belongs to starq and not to ERP — which is what a house mark needs to do.
