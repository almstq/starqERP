1c Tokens, components, copy rules.

One meaning per treatment

The old accent meant both "primary action" and "refused". Here the accent means only: this is yours, now. Everything else is a neutral step on the ramp, so a refusal cannot be mistaken for a fault. Role identity is carried by an icon and a word, never by colour.
