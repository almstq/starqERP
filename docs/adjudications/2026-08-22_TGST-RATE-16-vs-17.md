# Adjudication — TGST is 17%, not 16%

**Settled:** 22 August 2026 · **By:** Elder, against MIRA's own published circular
**Status:** CLOSED. Cite this document rather than re-arguing it.

---

## The dispute

Two research documents in the same corpus stated opposite rates for the tourism GST, and the
disagreement had gone unadjudicated while downstream product decisions were already built on it.

| Source | Claim |
|---|---|
| `research/reverse_engineer_EVETS.md:3` (and 636, 670, 794, 1067) | TGST is **17%** since 1 July 2025; EVETS marketing 16% is a live compliance defect |
| `research/Gemini_report_22Aug.docx` | "**[Verified Fact]** … Tourism Goods and Services Tax (TGST) is **16%**" — repeated in the PESTEL table and again in its raw-intelligence section |
| `research/Grok_report_22Aug.docx` | Neither — "tourism rates differentiated". Vague, but not wrong |
| `research/2026-08-22_ELDER_Integrated_Strategic_Architecture_v2.md` | States no rate |

## The finding

**TGST is 17%, effective 00:00 on 1 July 2025.** It was 16% before that date.

- MIRA issued a circular in December 2024 announcing the change, following the **seventh amendment
  to the Goods and Services Tax Act**.
- The rate applies where the *time of supply* falls on or after 1 July 2025 — time of supply being
  the earlier of invoice issue or payment received.
- MIRA directed GST-registered tourism operators to update billing and pricing systems and to
  ensure displayed prices reflect the revised rate.

Primary source: MIRA, *Circular issued regarding the change to TGST rates beginning from 1 July
2025* — https://www.mira.gov.mv/ContentItems/View/circular-issued-changes-to-tgst-rate
Corroborated by MIRA's GST overview page, Crowe Maldives, and contemporaneous trade press.

## Consequences — read these, they are not academic

**1. `reverse_engineer_EVETS.md` is CORRECT, and the differentiation thesis it carries stands.**
"Regulatory correctness as the brand" rests on a true premise. That thesis is safe to build on.

**2. The Gemini report is WRONG on a checkable published rate, and wrong under a `[Verified Fact]`
label.** That label is now known to be unreliable in that document. **Treat every `[Verified Fact]`
in `Gemini_report_22Aug.docx` as unverified until independently checked** — including the FX spread
(MVR 19.50–20.20 vs a 15.42 peg), the USD 250/month card limit, and the "70%+ of SME interaction
over WhatsApp" figure, none of which any other source corroborates.

**3. It is a live product requirement, not a marketing point.** A competitor shipping a hard-coded
16% is exactly what `reverse_engineer_EVETS.md` warns about: **never hard-code a tax rate.** Rates
must be *date-effective*, so that a return filed for June 2025 computes at 16% and July 2025 at 17%
**from the same code**. A system that merely swaps the constant to 17% has the identical defect
pointing the other way, and will misstate every prior-period correction.

**4. Anything asserting 16% as current is stale and must be corrected, not left standing.**
